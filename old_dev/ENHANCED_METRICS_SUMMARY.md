### 📊 **Metric Definitions:**
- **MSE**: Average squared differences between predicted and actual values
- **MAE**: Average absolute differences between predicted and actual values  
- **R² Score**: Proportion of variance in the target variable explained by the model
- **Precision**: Ratio of true positives to all positive predictions
- **Recall**: Ratio of true positives to all actual positives
- **F1-Score**: Harmonic mean of precision and recall
