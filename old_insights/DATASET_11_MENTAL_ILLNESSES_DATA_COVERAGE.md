## ⚠️ DATA QUALITY AND METHODOLOGY CONCERNS

### **Assessment Limitations**:
⚠️ 2008 snapshot may not reflect current status
⚠️ Regional aggregates hide country-level variation
⚠️ No quality grading of available data
⚠️ May not capture unpublished or grey literature
⚠️ Definition of "primary data" varies by context

### **Coverage Interpretation Issues**:
⚠️ High percentages don't guarantee data quality
⚠️ Zero percentages don't mean no cases exist
⚠️ Cultural factors affect reporting and recording
⚠️ Healthcare system differences impact data collection
⚠️ Language barriers may exclude relevant studies
