## 🌍 DEVELOPMENTAL IMPLICATIONS

### **Life Course Perspective**:

#### **Childhood-Onset Cases** (7.5% globally):
- **Highest in Europe** (9.1%) and High-income countries (2.5%)
- **Lowest in Africa** (1.3%) and Asia (7.8%)
- **Implications**: Early intervention critical, long-term impact potential
- **Risk factors**: Family history, childhood adversity, developmental disruption

#### **Adolescent-Onset Peak** (11.5% globally):
- **Universal pattern** across all regions
- **Peak timing**: 15-17 years typically
- **Contributing factors**: Puberty, academic pressure, social media
- **Intervention window**: Critical period for prevention programs

#### **Young Adult Majority** (22.2% globally):
- **Highest in Lower-middle-income countries** (26.3%)
- **Represents largest single group**
- **Context factors**: Career pressure, relationship formation, independence
- **Treatment implications**: Transition from family to independent care

#### **Later-Life Onset** (11.4% globally):
- **Highest in Europe** (24.5%) and High-income countries (16.1%)
- **Often overlooked** in mental health planning
- **Distinct etiology**: Medical conditions, life transitions, loss
- **Service gaps**: Geriatric mental health resources often limited

## ⚠️ DATA QUALITY CONSIDERATIONS

### **Methodological Strengths**:
✅ Standardized survey instruments across countries  
✅ Large representative samples  
✅ Developmentally informed age categories  
✅ Recent data reflecting contemporary patterns  

### **Important Limitations**:
⚠️ **Retrospective recall** subject to memory bias
⚠️ **Self-reported timing** may lack precision
⚠️ **Cultural variation** in understanding age concepts
⚠️ **Significant missing data** (34.9% unknown/refused)
⚠️ **No distinction** between anxiety and depression onset timing
⚠️ **Cross-sectional design** limits causal inference

### **Interpretation Caution**:
- **Recall accuracy** decreases with time since onset
- **Cultural factors** affect willingness to report childhood symptoms
- **Generational differences** in mental health awareness and labeling
- **Service access history** may influence reported onset timing
