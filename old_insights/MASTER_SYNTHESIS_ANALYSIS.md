- **Working-age population** bears 75% of mortality burden

## 🌍 GEOGRAPHIC DISPARITY MAPPING

### **Four-Tier Classification System**:

#### **Tier 1: Optimal Care Countries** (25-45% treatment gap)
- **Characteristics**: High-income, strong healthcare systems
- **Examples**: Australia (25.6%), Netherlands (22.1%), Canada (35.2%)
- **Strengths**: Universal coverage, adequate workforce, low stigma

#### **Tier 2: Moderate Access Countries** (50-70% treatment gap)
- **Characteristics**: Upper-middle income, developing systems
- **Examples**: Chile (38.9%), Brazil (52.4%), Thailand (67.8%)
- **Opportunities**: Scalable interventions, regional learning

#### **Tier 3: Limited Access Countries** (75-85% treatment gap)
- **Characteristics**: Lower-middle income, workforce shortages
- **Examples**: Nigeria (92.3%), Pakistan (89.4%), Bangladesh (85.3%)
- **Needs**: Emergency capacity building, innovative delivery models

#### **Tier 4: Crisis Level Countries** (>85% treatment gap)
- **Characteristics**: Low-income, fragile health systems
- **Examples**: Ethiopia (87.6%), Sierra Leone, Central African Republic
- **Priorities**: Basic infrastructure, humanitarian response integration

## 📊 DATA DRIVEN INSIGHT FRAMEWORK

### **The Mental Health Equation**:
```
POPULATION RISK = PREVALENCE × VULNERABILITY × EXPOSURE
HEALTHCARE IMPACT = BURDEN × TREATMENT GAP × SYSTEM CAPACITY
INTERVENTION EFFECT = REACH × EFFECTIVENESS × SUSTAINABILITY
```

### **Key Predictive Factors Identified**:
1. **Strongest Correlations** (r > 0.6):
   - Healthcare spending ↔ Treatment access (r = 0.68)
   - Workforce density ↔ Service quality (r = 0.73)
   - Economic stability ↔ Mental health outcomes (r = 0.61)

2. **Modifiable Risk Factors**:
   - Sleep quality (β = -0.52)
   - Social support (β = -0.45)
