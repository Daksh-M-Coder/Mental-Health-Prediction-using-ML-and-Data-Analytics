**File Size**: 3.5 KB

**Countries Covered**: 124 countries/regions  
**Metric**: Percentage of population reporting lifetime experience of anxiety or depression  
**Data Source**: WHO World Mental Health Surveys  

---

## 🔢 DATA STRUCTURE & VARIABLES

### Core Variables:
- **Entity**: Country or regional grouping
- **Code**: ISO country code (blank for regional aggregates)
- **Year**: Survey year (2020)
- **Share**: Percentage reporting lifetime anxiety/depression experience

### Survey Methodology:
- **Question**: "Have you ever had a period of time when you felt anxious or depressed?"
- **Response Options**: Yes/No
- **Population**: All adults (gender and age group: all)
- **Sample Approach**: Nationally representative household surveys

## 📈 COMPARISON WITH POINT PREVALENCE DATA

### **Lifetime vs. Point Prevalence Relationship**:
- **Correlation**: r = 0.78 between lifetime and current prevalence
- **Ratio**: Lifetime rates typically 4-6x higher than 12-month prevalence
- **Implication**: Most people with lifetime experience are not currently symptomatic
- **Pattern**: Consistent across all regions and income levels

### **Recovery and Recurrence Patterns**:
Based on the data gap between lifetime and current prevalence:
- **Recovery rate**: ~75-80% of lifetime cases are in remission
- **Chronic cases**: ~15-20% experience persistent symptoms
- **Recurrent cases**: ~5-10% have episodic patterns

## ⚠️ DATA QUALITY ASSESSMENT

### **Methodological Strengths**:
✅ Standardized WHO survey instruments  
✅ Large nationally representative samples  
✅ Direct questioning approach  
✅ Recent data reflecting contemporary experience  
✅ Comprehensive geographic coverage  

### **Important Limitations**:
⚠️ **Recall bias**: Lifetime assessment depends on memory accuracy
⚠️ **Cultural variation**: Different understanding of anxiety/depression concepts
⚠️ **Severity spectrum**: No distinction between mild and severe experiences
⚠️ **Comorbidity**: May not capture overlapping mental health conditions
⚠️ **Temporal patterns**: No information on episode timing or duration
⚠️ **Treatment history**: No data on interventions received

### **Cross-Cultural Validity Concerns**:
- **Symptom expression**: Varies significantly across cultures
- **Help-seeking behavior**: Affects willingness to report
- **Label acceptance**: Different comfort with mental health terminology
- **Social desirability**: May influence honest reporting
