# 🧠 MENTAL HEALTH DATASETS COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS
## Healthcare Data Analytics Internship Project

**Project Focus**: Mental Health Analytics & Insights  
**Total Datasets Analyzed**: 58 CSV files  
**Total Data Size**: ~35 MB  
**Analysis Date**: January 27, 2026  

---

## 🔍 DATASET CATEGORIZATION

### 1. **GLOBAL PREVALENCE DATASETS** (Core Epidemiological Data)
These datasets provide worldwide prevalence rates for major mental health conditions across countries and years.

### 2. **BURDEN OF DISEASE DATASETS** 
Measuring disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) and mortality rates from mental health conditions.

### 3. **DEMOGRAPHIC BREAKDOWN DATASETS**
Age-group and gender-specific prevalence data for detailed population analysis.

### 4. **TREATMENT & INTERVENTION DATASETS**
Coverage gaps, treatment approaches, and healthcare resource availability.

### 5. **SOCIAL & BEHAVIORAL DATASETS**
Public attitudes, help-seeking behaviors, and social support systems.

### 6. **HEALTHCARE INFRASTRUCTURE DATASETS**
Mental health workforce, facilities, and policy implementation data.

### 7. **INDIVIDUAL-LEVEL CLINICAL DATASETS**
Patient-level data with clinical scores and risk assessments.

## 📊 KEY INSIGHTS SUMMARY

### **Data Coverage**
- **Temporal Range**: 1990-2020 (30+ years of data)
- **Geographic Scope**: 195+ countries and territories
- **Population Groups**: All ages, both genders, various demographics
- **Conditions Covered**: Depression, Anxiety, Bipolar, Schizophrenia, Eating Disorders

### **Data Quality Indicators**
- Standardized metrics using WHO and IHME methodologies
- Age-standardized rates for international comparability
- Consistent coding systems (ISO country codes)
- Regular temporal intervals (annual data)

### **Analytical Opportunities**
1. **Trend Analysis**: Long-term prevalence changes (1990-2020)
2. **Geographic Comparison**: Country-level disparities
3. **Demographic Analysis**: Age and gender differences
4. **Healthcare Gap Assessment**: Treatment vs. prevalence ratios
5. **Policy Impact Evaluation**: Correlation with mental health policies
6. **Risk Factor Identification**: Social determinants of mental health
